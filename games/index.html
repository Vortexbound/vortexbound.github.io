About

Vortexbound Systems is a two-person studio working out of a small apartment full of monitors and cold coffee. We build games about machines that shouldn't work, places that shouldn't exist, and systems that quietly fall apart.

Everything here is made, tested, and shipped by us — art, code, sound, and the occasional 3am bug fix.
